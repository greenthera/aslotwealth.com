Cookie Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

1. What Cookies Are

Cookies are small text files stored in your browser when you visit a website. This policy explains which cookies the Aslot Wealth Advisor website uses and how you can control them.

2. Strictly Necessary

A preference cookie remembers your cookie choices for 182 days. The map in our contact section is also an always-enabled service. The embedded map connects to Google Maps, which may use its own cookies.

3. Analytics

With your permission, we use Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity (through Google Tag Manager) to understand how visitors use the website so we can improve it. These tools stay off until you click Accept or allow Analytics in Cookie settings. Advertising cookies are not used. You can browse the website and contact us without accepting analytics.

4. Cookies We Use

5. Manage Your Choices

You can accept or decline analytics in the cookie dialog and change your choice at any time using Cookie settings in the footer. Turning analytics off clears matching cookies the website can access and reloads the page if needed to stop tracking that has already loaded. This does not remove cookies stored on an external provider's domain; you can delete those in your browser settings.

6. Enquiry Information

Cookie choices are separate from the details you send through our contact form. Read our Privacy Policy to learn how we use those details.

7. Contact Us
